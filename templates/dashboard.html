🧪 Control experiment — why this isn't curve-fitting

The Baltic Capesize index is a daily broker survey; panellists anchor on the previous print, so its returns are strongly autocorrelated and genuinely forecastable. BDRY is a liquid, arbitraged ETF over the same underlying. We ran the identical method on it. If we scored well there too, we would be claiming to beat a futures market — a red flag, not a feature.
